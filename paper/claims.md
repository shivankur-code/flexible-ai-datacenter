# Claims ledger (input to paper-orchestra)

Every number here comes from a committed script. Format: **claim** → evidence (file, row or column) → figure.
Results CSVs live in `results/` (gitignored, regenerated by the listed script).
Shared setup: RTS-GMLC 2020, lines derated to 0.6x, one 300 MW LLM-inference facility, 364 days,
48 h-lookahead rolling DC-OPF (HiGHS), default `configs/fleet_default.yaml`.

## Brief placeholders (current values)
- **X% (congestion cost):** co-optimised flexibility cuts grid congestion cost by **3.8%** at the wind-pocket bus 303
  (2.0% at 122, 0.7% at 313; +0.4% at coal-heavy 101). → `results/p3/summary.csv` `d_congestion_pct` → Fig. 3 (313)
- **Y% (emissions):** following locational marginal emissions cuts the facility's hosting emissions by **9.6–12.3%**
  at buses 122/303/313 (5.7% at 101). Cost-optimal co-optimisation cuts them 2.4–3.2%. → `summary.csv` `d_emis_pct_hosting` → Fig. 4
- Z%, N×: phase 4. Figure 1: phase 5.

## Phase 1: grid
- At nameplate ratings RTS-GMLC is lightly congested: 4,114 binding line-hours/yr vs 22,328 at 0.6x. → `results/p1/summary.csv`
- The mean marginal emission rate is 0.71 t/MWh vs an average of 0.42 t/MWh. Coal ($23/MWh, 1.03 t/MWh) undercuts CC gas ($29, 0.40). → `results/p1/summary.csv`, `tests/test_opf.py::test_rts_loader`
- LMPs and LMEs from the optimal basis match finite differences (tests). → `tests/test_opf.py`

## Phase 2: fleet → envelope
- Under p99 TTFT 1 s / TPOT 100 ms, replicas provisioned at 80% of capacity can be capped only to about 70% of TDP; below that, SLO capacity collapses (conversation 42%, code 20% at a 60% cap). → `results/p2/power_cap_tradeoff.csv` → Fig. 2a
- Sheddable power within the SLO is **4.9% of facility load** (10% with SLOs loosened 2x). Deferrable batch energy is **19.8%** at the default batch fill. → `results/p2/envelope_summary_300mw.csv`, `flex_sweeps.csv` → Fig. 2b, 2c
- Batch work is most efficient at a 50% power cap: 4.6 vs 6.0 J/token uncapped. → `results/p2/fleet_batch_table.csv`

## Phase 3: grid value
- Hosting the inflexible facility costs $24.7–34.4M/yr and 657–1,111 kt CO2/yr (0.48–0.81 t/MWh), depending on bus. → `summary.csv` `hosting_*` (rows `*_inflexible`)
- Co-optimised flexibility cuts hosting cost by 2.8–8.4%. At bus 313, the split is shift-only −2.1% and shed-only −1.8% (sum −3.9%). → rows `313_coopt_shift`, `313_coopt_shed`
- Shedding's emissions benefit is the energy saved: 20.5 GWh × LME ≈ the 1.5% shed-only reduction. → `313_coopt_shed`
- Signal paradox: price-following and co-opt raise emissions +0.9% at bus 101 while cutting cost 2.8%. Average-CI following is emissions-neutral there (+0.002%) despite re-timing ~250 GWh of load. → rows `101_*` → Fig. 4
- Average-CI following, shift-only, raises emissions +0.47% at bus 313. → `313_avg_ci_shift`
- With a $50/t grid carbon price, price-following, LME-following and co-opt converge (−22.3 to −22.5 kt/yr). → rows `313_*_cp50`
- Bus 207 (radial, 105 MW line at 0.6x) cannot host 300 MW: 156 GWh/yr of involuntary shedding. Co-opt cuts it 36%, while avg-CI and LME following raise it 43% and 38%. → rows `207_*` `d_grid_shed_gwh`

## Caveats to state
- Single facility (price-taker regime). Multi-site and larger facilities come in phase 5.
- Perfect day-ahead foresight of load and renewables. No unit commitment (Pmin relaxed except nuclear).
- Fleet parameters marked ASSUMPTION in the config, not yet swept.
- End-of-year backlog is charged at mean LMP/LME (≤ 650 MWh, < 0.25% of shifted energy).
